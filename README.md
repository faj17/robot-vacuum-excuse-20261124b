One original funny excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot (101 characters):

> My sensors found the dust, filed it under decorative, and I have decided to respect its life choices.

The setup is rooted in the vacuum's own world — its sensors found the dust and filed it, the way a little machine would categorise the world. The turn is the last few words: the dust is not dirt to clean but a resident whose life choices the vacuum respects, so the excuse lands on the first read. It sounds like the vacuum itself talking, a small machine keeping a straight face, not the owner putting words in its mouth.
